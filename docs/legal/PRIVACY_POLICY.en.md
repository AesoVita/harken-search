Harken Search Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 28, 2026

Introduction

XD Network Inc. (hereinafter referred to as “we,” “us,” or “XD”), as the operator of Harken Search, recognizes the importance of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, protect, and delete your personal information when you use Harken Search, and how you may exercise your rights in relation to such information.

Before using the Product, please read and fully understand this Privacy Policy. Your use of the Product or related services, or your clicking “Agree,” constitutes application of this Policy. If you do not agree to any provision of this Policy, or cannot accurately understand any provision, please stop using the Product.

I. How We Collect and Use Personal Information

We process personal information as necessary to provide Product functions, ensure security, handle your requests, and comply with legal obligations. Unless otherwise required by law or unless we have obtained your separate consent, we will not use your personal information for purposes unrelated to those described above.

1. Harken Search Local Functions

Harken Search may scan, index, search, preview, and manage audio assets locally on your device and may process file paths, file names, audio metadata, waveforms, loudness, acoustic features, tags, descriptions, search terms, filter criteria, similarity results, transcription text, temporary files, and local databases. This information is used to establish and maintain your local sound library and to provide search, preview, analysis, transcription, and metadata-management functions, and is generally not transmitted to XD’s servers.

If you use the metadata-writing function, Harken Search may modify metadata in the original audio files. Please verify the relevant files and make appropriate backups before performing such an operation.

When you use update-checking, automatic-update, or official-download functions, Harken Search will, based on your device’s operating-system type, first access the publicly available update manifest or release address on GitHub to obtain the installation or update package applicable to your device. If the GitHub connection is unavailable or does not function properly, Harken Search may switch to a backup download address, such as OSS. The determination of update applicability and integrity verification are performed locally on your device.

If you yourself add a LAN NAS or other local-network location as a sound library or storage location, the Product may process file paths, file names, audio metadata, waveforms, tags, indexes, transcription text, and local-database information at that location. Such information is used to provide search, preview, analysis, and metadata-management functions on the storage you designate, is generally stored on that storage or on your device, and is not uploaded to XD’s servers as a result. NAS addresses, accounts, and passwords are configured and kept by you; we will not require you to submit those credentials to us.

2. Feedback, Customer Service, Security Reports, and Community Collaboration

When you voluntarily submit feedback, customer-service requests, crash reports, security reports, Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, or other community content, we may process the name or nickname, contact details, feedback description, attachments, system and version information, logs, crash reports, and communications that you choose to submit. We use such information to respond to requests, troubleshoot issues, maintain project security, and administer the community.

Please do not submit unnecessary personal information, trade secrets, unauthorized materials, account passwords, API Keys, access tokens, signing private keys, or other sensitive credentials in feedback, logs, public community content, or attachments. Submissions, comments, commit records, names, email addresses, and attachments on public code-hosting platforms may remain publicly visible for an extended period in accordance with the applicable platform’s rules.

IV. Data Storage and Processing Locations

Harken Search is designed primarily for local processing. Audio files, file paths, indexes, tags, metadata, transcription text, temporary files, local databases, caches, local logs, and configurations that you scan, index, search, preview, or manage are generally stored on your device.

Audio, indexes, and databases on a LAN NAS or other local-network location that you designate are generally stored on that storage, and their retention is controlled by you.

When you use third-party services such as GitHub, OSS, CDNs, code-hosting services, or official download and update services, the relevant providers may process necessary network-access information and content that you voluntarily submit in the locations where they operate or in other locations, in accordance with their own terms of service and privacy policies. XD does not control the storage locations, processing methods, or retention periods of such third-party services.

We will provide the notices and implement the authorization, protection, and security measures required by applicable law with respect to personal information under our control or processed by us.

V. Retention of Information

Unless otherwise required by law or necessary to handle disputes, complaints, or security incidents, we retain personal information only for the minimum period necessary to achieve the purposes described in this Policy.

Audio files, indexes, tags, metadata, transcription text, temporary files, local databases, caches, local logs, and configurations are generally stored on your device, and their retention is controlled by you. If you use the metadata-writing function, changes to original audio files also remain on your device and are retained under your control.

Audio, indexes, and databases on a LAN NAS or other local-network location that you designate are generally stored on that storage, and their retention is controlled by you.

VII. Your Rights

To the extent provided by applicable law, you have the right, with respect to personal information that we actually process and that relates to you, to access, copy, correct, supplement, delete, withdraw consent, and obtain an explanation of our processing rules.

Harken Search is designed primarily for local processing and has no account system. The extent to which you can exercise the rights above depends on whether the relevant information is under our control and whether we can reasonably associate that information with you.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

Audio files, file paths, file names, audio metadata, waveforms, tags, descriptions, search terms, filter criteria, similarity results, transcription text, temporary files, local databases, caches, local logs, Crash Dumps, and configurations that you scan, index, search, preview, or manage on your device are generally stored only on your device. You may access, correct, export, or delete such information through Product functions, system settings, or by manually deleting local files.

If you use the metadata-writing function, the relevant changes are made to the original audio files on your device and should also be accessed, corrected, or otherwise handled by you locally.

Such information is generally not uploaded to XD’s servers, and we generally cannot directly access, correct, delete, or restore it.

For information that you voluntarily submit to our feedback, customer-service, or security-reporting systems, including feedback content, contact details, nicknames, operating-system information, RAM, CPU, GPU, product version, and logs or crash reports that you voluntarily attach, you may request access, copying, correction, or deletion through the contact details set out in this Policy.

If you have not provided contact details or other identifying information, we may be unable to locate the relevant records accurately. When submitting a request, you may provide the submission time, title, relevant keywords, or feedback number to assist our search.

3. Network-Access Logs

For network-access logs generated by the official website, OSS, CDN, or other download and update services under our control, you may submit a request for access, correction, or deletion in accordance with applicable law. The scope of any response will depend on whether the logs remain within the retention period, whether they can be accurately matched to you, whether they are under our control, and whether statutory retention or security-audit requirements apply.

Logs retained independently by third-party service providers may need to be handled in accordance with their rules.

4. Information Held by Third-Party Services

Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, comments, or commit records that you submit to GitHub or other code-hosting platforms are processed by the relevant platform under its rules. Please exercise your rights through the channels provided by that third party. We generally cannot access, correct, or delete information controlled by such third parties on your behalf.
