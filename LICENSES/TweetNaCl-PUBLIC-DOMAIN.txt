TweetNaCl public-domain provenance notice

Original authors:
Daniel J. Bernstein, Bernard van Gastel, Wesley Janssen, Tanja Lange,
Peter Schwabe, and Sjaak Smetsers.

Upstream project: https://tweetnacl.cr.yp.to/
Upstream software page: https://tweetnacl.cr.yp.to/software.html

The upstream TweetNaCl implementation is released into the public domain.
Aris carries an altered verify-only subset under `src/ed25519/`; keypair
generation and signing functions were removed. The source files retain the
original author attribution and identify the local alteration.
